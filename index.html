How To Use This Journal

In the book of Exodus we find one of the most pivotal stories in the Bible — Moses and the burning bush. During this what must have been intense encounter, God commands Moses to remove his sandals because, “The place on which you are standing is holy ground.” (Exodus 3:5)

What made the place Holy? Was it that particular land? That particular piece of earth set apart? No, I think it was Holy, simply because God was present. God had instructed Moses to prepare himself to be in His presence.

But why sandals? By commanding Moses to remove them, God was placing him in position of vulnerability and humility, placing Moses in a space where he could be ready to hear what God had to say to him.

Quiet is a simple, Biblically-oriented devotional journal designed to help you connect with God and live a more content, grateful, and connected life. It uses a four-step strategy:

Open to the day’s page each morning from 1 December to Christmas Day, or whenever you have five quiet minutes. Work through Give Worry, Give Gratitude, Reflect and Pray in order, or land wherever you need to most. There’s no wrong way to use this — it’s here to hold what a busy December doesn’t always leave room for.
By giving over our worries and practicing gratitude we are in essence, taking off our shoes and preparing ourselves to hear what God has to say to us through his word, each day.
Then after reflecting on it, this process gives our prayer time more meaning, we Give God our worries, Give thanks for the things we are grateful for, and lean into what He is telling us through His Word.

The Four Steps

The Four Steps

Give Worry

Name whatever’s sitting on you today. Put it on the page instead of carrying it all day.

Give Gratitude

Notice something good, even a small one. It doesn’t have to outweigh the hard stuff to count.

Reflect

One passage from the Advent story, one question that connects it to your own day.

Pray

A short line to start with, then space to keep going in your own words.
